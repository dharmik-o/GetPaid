# E1: closing an invoice without PayPal (solo build and test, 12 Nov 2026)

Branch `feat/E1-outside-payments`. Decisions are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` ("E1 choices").

## What was built

- Migration `033_invoice_payments.sql`: append-only `invoice_payments` with RLS, one row per PayPal payment id.
- `POST /api/invoices/[id]/payments` (record a payment): PayPal's record-payment call first, then the invoice is read back and our status and amount follow PayPal's, through the same code the webhook uses.
- `POST /api/invoices/[id]/cancel` now needs a reason from a fixed list.
- `POST /api/projects/[id]/complete`: Mark project complete.
- Screens: Record a payment (project cards, invoice history, the reply card's "Record their payment"), Cancel invoice with a reason, Mark project complete with a Completed badge, "Paid by" under the status in the history, a "Paid by" column in the lawyer pack, and timeline rows.
- Spec, DESIGN and manual wording updated.

## Not checked, and why

- **Real PayPal sandbox.** Everything ran against msw and the E2E fake. PayPal's page for record-payment does not say whether `PayPal-Request-Id` is accepted on it, nor what the invoice's `payments` block looks like after a manual payment; I read only the status and the paid amount from the invoice, as the webhook already does. Owner: record one payment on a sandbox invoice and look at it in PayPal.
- **Look of the forms** in dark mode and on a phone: built from existing classes, not looked at by me.
- **The PDF manual** (`GetPaid-User-Manual.pdf`) is not regenerated: only `docs/help/manual.md` changed.

## Decisions the owner may want to look at

- Retainer-cycle invoices cannot be closed this way yet (an auto-pay catch-up invoice has to reactivate the subscription, which only the webhook does).
- The date-received box starts at the browser's date, the server checks the account's time zone.
