## What the screenshots found, and what was fixed

1. **A checkbox drew as a full-width yellow bar** (Client page, "This client is a business"): `.field input`, `.form > input` and `.editor-form input` forced `width: 100%` on it. Those rules now leave checkboxes and radios out.
2. **Checkboxes and radios in the schedule form were 16px and 14px,** not 24px: `.schedule-group input { width: auto }`. Same fix. Every checkbox and radio on nine pages now measures 24×24.
3. **A milestone card said "Invoiced" while Home said "6 days overdue".** Overdue isn't stored, so the card now uses the invoice log's own rule (an unpaid invoice past its date, in the client's zone) to show "Overdue 6d" and the OVERDUE stamp.
4. **The Outbox count sat under its label on the phone bar.** It now rides on the icon.
5. **The paid stamp covered the amount in the Home ledger.** In a ledger row it lands beside the status words.
6. **The light-mode PART PAID stamp was 2.94:1.** It now uses the next ochre step (4.82:1).
7. **A selector, not an assertion:** `tests/e2e/demo.spec.ts` and `z-paid-stamp.spec.ts` found a milestone by table row or `.milestone-table`; the milestones are cards now (`article`, `.milestone-grid`). Same assertions. `tests/timeline-view.test.ts` and `tests/theme-contrast.test.ts` now name the new words (Email, Client reply) and the new dark surface (#161616).

## What is not the same as the Stitch screens, on purpose

- No sample wording: no "click-tracking", "verified settlement link", "Tone: …", or double space after a currency code.
- No "+18.4% vs previous month": the app has no such number.
- The invoice log keeps its table (every column is required); the Timeline has no "Promise date" chip or "Adjust schedule" button (no data or form for them). Both are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` and `docs/NEEDS_YOU.md`.
