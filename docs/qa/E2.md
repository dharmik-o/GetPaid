# E2: deleting and archiving projects (solo build and test, 12 Nov 2026)

Branch `feat/E2-delete-project`. Decisions are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` ("E2 choices").

## What was built

- Migration `034_project_archive.sql`: `projects.archived_at`.
- Project page "Danger zone": Archive or Restore, and Delete with the typed name, the list of open PayPal invoices and holds, the "Cancel them in PayPal too" tick, and a note that invoice PDFs stay on disk.
- Projects page: "Show archived" and an Archived chip. Archived projects are also left out of the client's page, Home's figures, Upcoming, the Maintenance list, and the scheduler's planning.
- `DELETE /api/projects/[id]`, `POST /api/projects/[id]/archive`, `POST /api/projects/[id]/restore`, `DELETE /api/milestones/[id]`, `DELETE /api/retainers/[id]`.
- A Delete button on a saved milestone with no invoice or hold, and on a retainer that never charged.
- Spec, DESIGN and manual updated.

## Not checked, and why

- **An authorized hold voided through the real PayPal sandbox:** the test covers a hold waiting for approval (no PayPal call) and a failing invoice cancel; `voidHold` itself is the existing, already-tested function.
- **Look of the Danger zone** in dark mode and on a phone: built from existing classes, not looked at by me.
- **The calendar** still shows an archived project's dates (decision logged).
- **Archived projects and replies:** a client's reply on an archived project's invoice still appears in the Outbox's replies; archiving stops reminders, not mail that arrives.

## For the owner

- Archiving removes the project's invoices from Home's recovery totals, as it hides the project from Home. If you would rather keep recovered money counted, say so.
- `npm run db:migrate` applies 033 and 034 on the dev database.
