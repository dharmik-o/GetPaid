## Rules checked (plan section 2)

- **Rule 4 (legal text):** the clauses are fixed text in `lib/agreement-template.ts`; no AI is involved anywhere; a law is cited only through `pickCitation` (approved, conditions met). The builder says "This is not legal advice." The lawyer review is in `docs/NEEDS_YOU.md`.
- **Rule 3 (money):** deposit and balance are integer maths (BigInt, rounded half up once); the late fee is cents; the interest rate is basis points.
- **Rule 8 (service role) and rule 9 (logging):** not used; logs carry event names and the agreement id only, never the document, names or options.
- **Rule 5 (nothing sends without approval):** nothing is sent in this step. Sending is C3.
- **Section 11 Don'ts:** a sent agreement can't change (database trigger); no AI writes clauses.
